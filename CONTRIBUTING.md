# Contributing

Thanks for helping keep these numbers honest.

- **Found a wrong number or unfair setup?** Open a "Result or method concern" issue with what you ran and what you saw.
- **Re-ran the benchmark?** Open an issue with your config, models and scores. We will link it.
- **Fixing a typo or link?** Send a PR.

Before a PR: check that `README.md` and `RESULTS.md` agree, and that links work. Score changes need a harness re-run (see [bench/README.md](bench/README.md)). The PR template asks you to disclose AI use.
